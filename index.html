Our Mission

Restoring balance to our marine ecosystems through science, advocacy, and direct action.

Why the Ocean Matters

The ocean covers 70% of our planet's surface, yet it remains one of the least protected areas. It regulates our climate, generates 50% of the oxygen we breathe, and provides food for billions.

Founded in 2015, Blue Planet Initiative has mobilized volunteers across 80 coastal nations to fight plastic pollution and overfishing.

We believe that a healthy ocean means a healthy planet. Our data-driven approach ensures that every dollar donated makes a tangible impact on marine conservation.
